Privacy Policy

Minecraft Backups is a privately operated backup integration that uses Google Drive to store backups of a self-hosted Minecraft server. The integration is intended for personal use only.

Google Drive Access

The integration uses Google OAuth to authorize rclone to upload, access, and manage backup files in the authorized Google Drive account. Access is limited to the permissions granted during the Google OAuth authorization process.

Data Storage

Minecraft backup archives are generated by the self-hosted backup system and uploaded to the authorized user's Google Drive account. Google OAuth credentials are stored locally and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Data Sharing

Minecraft backup files and Google account information are not sold or intentionally shared with third parties. Google Drive processes and stores backup data as part of providing its storage service.

Data Retention

Older backup archives are automatically removed according to the configured backup retention policy. Backup files may also be manually deleted from Google Drive at any time.
